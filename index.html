About Me

Academic background and research interests.

I am a postgraduate student in Atmospheric Sciences and Meteorology at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, with an academic background in Geological and Earth Sciences.

My research interests focus on atmospheric physics, meteorology, climatology and the application of numerical models to understand atmospheric and climate processes.

Through my research activities, I work with meteorological and greenhouse gas observations, climate datasets, statistical analysis and scientific programming using Python.
